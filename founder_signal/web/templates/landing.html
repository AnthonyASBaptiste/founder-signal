How It Works

1

Drop material in your inbox

Emails, meeting transcripts, voice notes, and screen recordings go into your inbox folder. A local cron job picks them up on a schedule you control.

2

Ollama extracts commitments

Runs locally on your machine via Ollama — nothing leaves your device. Commitments are extracted and reconciled against your existing ledger.

3

Review in your cockpit

Sign in to Founder Signal online to see all commitments, proposed changes, and what needs your attention today. Your data lives in MongoDB Atlas, shared between local and cloud.

Built for Founders

📧

Email Ingestion

Pull commitments from your email via himalaya or drop .eml files in your inbox.

🎙️

Voice & Video

Transcribe meeting recordings and screen captures with openai-whisper, then extract commitments.

🔄

Change Reconciliation

When dates or owners shift, proposed changes are flagged for your review — accept or reject with one click.

📊

Morning Briefing

Do-now, coming-up, waiting-on, and needs-clarification lanes so you know exactly where to focus.

🔒

Single Founder Login

Password-protected access to your commitment ledger. Your email contents stay private.

🪵

Processing Log

See every file processed — filename, source, date received, date processed, status, and commitments extracted.

Privacy First

Extraction runs on your machine via Ollama. Nothing is sent to a cloud LLM. The Render frontend only reads from your shared MongoDB Atlas database — it never processes raw email content.
